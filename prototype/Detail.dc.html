About This Surfside Beach House

A three-bedroom beach house in Surfside Beach, Texas, with a long covered deck that looks straight out over the Gulf of Mexico.

Made for families and friends who want the Gulf at the front door, breakfast on the deck and enough room for everyone to spread out after a day on the sand. The beach is about a 5-minute walk and Jetty Park about 10.

Inside there is a full kitchen with a dishwasher, a round dining table, a living room with two big sofas, Wi-Fi with a dedicated workspace, central air and a washer and dryer. Parking on site is free, with an EV charger. Check-in is self check-in with a lockbox.
